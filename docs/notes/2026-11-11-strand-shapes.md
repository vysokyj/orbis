# 2026-11-11 — How DAZ hands strands to Iray (#79)

Picks up [2026-11-06](2026-11-06-morgan-hair-strands.md): Morgan Hair's
strand gloss stays more saturated and placed differently in Iray than in
Octane, and no Octane pin (roughness, anisotropy, specular) moved it. The
issue asked for a controlled strand scene from free content first. Before
any render the `.mi` was read (docs first, decompose before fitting) — and
it changed the question.

## Iray never sees a round strand from Morgan Hair

Morgan's strands in the `.mi` of Calib-Hair-Teens-ng-fx0 (local, store
content; numbers only): one `tagged trilist`, 3.0 M vertices, `p n t 2 2`.
Decoding the binary block: every strand point has **two** vertices about
0.11 – 0.20 mm apart with the **same normal**, joined to the next point's
pair by two triangles (plus one unused zero vertex per point). That is a
flat ribbon one Line Width wide (Line Start / End Width 0.2 / 0.1), not a
tube: the 2026-11-06 note's "thin tubes whose UV u runs around the
strand" was read from u stepping by a few thousandths across the pair. u
does run across the strand, so the quarter-turned anisotropy (#52) still
holds.

What decides the shape is the figure's **Line Tessellation** modifier
(`studio/modifier/line_tessellation`, Viewport / Render Line Tessellation
Sides). Morgan carries 2 / 2, dForce Corporate (Calib-Hair) 3 / 3, the free
dForce Mohawk 0 / 1.

## Calib-Strand: synthetic strands in DSON

`scripts/dev/make-strand-scenes.py` writes the strands into the scene
itself (a `geometry_library` with a `polyline_list`, all-zero UVs as the
Mohawk's), from calib/scenes/Calib-L-Distant-45 without floor and cubes:

- row A: one thick strand per material (Line Width 20);
- row B: the same as 24-sided smooth mesh tubes, radius 1 cm, UV u around;
- rows C / D: 3000 thin strands (Morgan's widths), horizontal / vertical;
- materials: diffuse grey, white gloss isotropic / anisotropic (Morgan's
  0.735, rotation 0.25), Morgan's gloss (glossiness mode, colours, effect
  2, no diffuse), Morgan's Strands without top coat and maps; every
  channel shown.

`.mi` probes (row A alone, scratch scenes):

| Line Tessellation | `.mi` object |
|---|---|
| no modifier | `hair`, `degree 3 bspline`, radius 1 for width 20 (= width / 20 cm), one extra control point at each end |
| modifier on a plain node | ignored: DAZ builds the modifier stack on figures only — tessellated strands are written as `figure` nodes |
| Render sides 1 / 2 / 3, Viewport 0 | `hair` — the `.mi` export reads the Viewport sides |
| sides 2 (both) | 3 vertices per point (one unused), a ribbon 2 cm wide (y), normal +z |
| sides 3 | 7 per point: three flat faces, normals (0, 0, −1), (0, 0.87, 0.5) …, circumradius 1 cm |
| sides 6 | 72 triangles for 6 segments (hexagonal prism) |

Ribbon orientation on plain polylines (four strands: along x, y, z, an arc
in the xy plane): the normal is the object's +z made perpendicular to the
strand; a strand along z gets −y. Morgan's ribbons (generated by the dForce
render hair generator) follow no fixed axis: |n · axis projected| has a
median of 0.66 – 0.73 for x, y and z alike — each generated hair has its
own frame. An `.mi` of the free Mohawk with sides 2 would show that frame,
but the headless export of the preset wrote the figure without its hair
(after "No camera for mi export" was fixed with a scripted camera); left
open.

Two conversion bugs the `.mi` of Calib-Strand shows (filed, not fixed
here): DAZ's auto Iray ground sits at the scene's lowest point (−45 cm),
`iray.rs` at 0 (#119); `full_refraction_reflection_enable` is off for a
glossiness-mode material with refraction (Morgan's base), `iray.rs` writes
it on (#120). The `.mi` fixture stays out of git until those are settled.

## Octane side

The export takes the dump's polylines either way: every Calib-Strand
variant renders the same curves in Octane (round, 2× Line Width) and only
the light differs. Rendered enhanced-final, 256 samples, 1920 × 1080:
Calib-Strand, -Side, -Dome, -T2, -T3, -T2-Dome. The thick curve (row A) is
twice the mesh tube's diameter (row B), as `LINE_WIDTH_SCALE` says. The
vertical patches with gloss only (D3, D4) are black under the sun — the
reflection cone misses the camera, as it should for a round fibre.

## What the Iray batch decides

Iray references of the six scenes put Octane's round curves against Iray's
fibres (Calib-Strand), ribbons (-T2) and prisms (-T3) under one sun, and
under the dome (-Dome, -T2-Dome: Morgan's case). If Octane's curves match
Iray's fibres but not its ribbons, Morgan's gloss gap is the strand shape,
and the export should hand Octane what DAZ hands Iray (ribbons / prisms as
meshes for tessellated hair) — for generated hair that needs the frame rule
first. Rows A / B also give the width: Iray's fibre and ribbon are 1×,
the export 2×.

## Process

- `daz-dump.sh` keys the dump by the scene path as given: dump with
  absolute paths when `calib-variants.sh` gets absolute paths, or the
  export finds no dump.
- A headless DAZ has no `MainWindow`; the `.mi` export needs a camera in
  the scene (a preset load has none).
- Timings: `.mi` export of the six scenes ≈ 15 s, dumps 4 – 5 s each,
  Octane 256 samples ≈ 30 s per scene.
