## Genesis 9 eye: translucency as colour (#66)

Genesis 9 keeps the whole eye in one PBRSkin surface per eye (a closed
ball). `c7cbd7a` already keeps it off the random-walk skin path. Left:
with its translucency it still went through Universal's transmission and
the whole eye glowed milky (Gladiator, Julius 9). Iray scatters that
translucency — the iris — just under the surface (scattering distance
0.15 mm), so it shows as colour, not light through the ball.

Committed unverified (tests pass, no Octane / Iray measurement):

- `src/material.rs`: `eye_volume` (is_eye + PBRSkin + translucency > 0 + not
  thin) sets transmission to 0 and bakes the diffuse and Translucency Color
  maps into one albedo, `Textures::blend` (weights 1 − t and t, mixed in
  linear light, sRGB-encoded at the diffuse's size). The weights are a guess
  from the PBRSkin layering; not yet checked against `pbr_skin.mdl`.
- `src/layered_image.rs`: layers of another size than the map sit at their
  own size, scaled with the map (Julius 9's 4096 × 2048 iris layer fills the
  top half of his 4096 × 4096 eye map, as DAZ's temp image for Iray shows;
  stretched over all of it the irises missed the eyes' UVs). The new test
  compares with that DAZ temp image and skips without DAZ content.
- `scripts/dev/daz-build-eye-scene.dsa`: Calib-Eye with another figure in
  Genesis 8's place (`DONE_FILE=<out>.done scripts/dev/daz-script.sh
  $PWD/scripts/dev/daz-build-eye-scene.dsa <Calib-Eye.duf> <figure.duf>
  <out.duf> -- <content dirs>`). Untested end to end. Scenes with store
  figures stay local.

sclera / pupil. Check the blend weights against `pbr_skin.mdl`. Then record
