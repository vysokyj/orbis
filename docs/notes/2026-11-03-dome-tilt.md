# 2026-11-03 Dome tilt and spot shapes (#65)

The texture environment's image `transform` is a UV transform (a 3D
rotation does nothing), so Dome Orientation X / Z were left out. Probe 110
(pins): Spherical projection = type 77, pins `transform` (3D, node 67),
`positionType` (1 world, 3 object = default), `useRestAttributes`; the image
texture's `projection` pin defaults to UVW (78).

Scene Calib-L-Mi-Dome-turned (Dome Rotation 30, Orientation X 10, Y 20,
Z 5, Iray ground in Manual mode at 12 cm). Octane 256 samples, 45 s each.
Compared as correlation of luminance in the top 40 % of the frame (the sky;
below it Iray's opaque ground differs, a separate item of #65), Iray vs
Octane.

| Export | sky corr |
|---|---|
| no tilt, U shift 0.25 + (30 − 20) / 360 (as before) | 0.78 |
| projection YXZ (−x, −y, −z), the "sphere turns by the inverse" guess | −0.22 |
| YXZ (+x, −y, +z) / (+x, −y, −z) | 0.70 / 0.71 |
| ZXY (−x, y, z) / (x, y, −z) / (−x, y, −z) | 0.52 / 0.86 / 0.32 |
| **ZXY (x, y, z)**, U shift 0.25 + 30 / 360 | **0.95** |

Checks that settled the convention:
- Projection with rotation 0 and the same U shift renders identically to no
  projection (corr 1.000): the projection keeps the map's base frame.
- Projection Y +20° with U shift 0.25 + 30 / 360 = no projection with
  0.25 + (30 − 20) / 360 (corr 1.000): the projection's rotation turns the
  lookup direction itself, not the sphere by its inverse.
- So Iray's `environment_function_orientation` Rz · Rx · Ry goes in as is:
  rotation (x, y, z), Octane order ZXY (4).

Process: the first sign sweep guessed the inverse and kept Y wrong, so no
variant matched; isolating Y first (two renders) fixed the frame.

## Spot shapes

A spot with `Light Geometry` Rectangle went out as an analytic disk of
max(width, height). Probe 111 (pins of 402): `quadAnalyticLightSize` and
`diskAnalyticLightSize`, both x, y in the light's XY plane with the normal
+Z — the frame the disk already used. Rect → QUAD (type 0) of width ×
height.

Calib-L-Mi-Spot-rect (20 × 40 cm, 250 cm above the floor, the camera above
it sees the emitter's dark back). Octane 256 samples, 22 s. Along the
floor's middle row:

| | peak | half-max radius (px) | mean, centre 600 px |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iray | 126 | 204 | 46.4 |
| disk 40 cm (before) | 111 | 61* | 36.9 |
| **quad 20 × 40** | 129 | 195 | 40.6 |

\* the disk's own silhouette covers the row to 103 px. The emitter now has
Iray's outline; the beam's falloff is the fitted cone as before (#7).
Outside the floor Iray's frame is white, Octane's black — not looked into.
