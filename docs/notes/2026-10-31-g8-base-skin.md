# 2026-10-31 — Genesis 8 base skin variants (#28)

The Genesis 8 Female base skin (Iray Uber, mono SSS, Base Color Effect 1,
SSS Direction −0.65, Translucency Weight 0.5) on Calib-Skin's body and
lights: `Calib-Skin-Base-{front,back,hdri}` and one channel changed per
variant. Iray references on the Iray Server at 5000 samples
(`~/Library/Caches/orbis/calib/iray-full`), Octane renders of the current
export (Enhanced, 400 samples). Regions as in `skin-compare.py`; the back
light leaves the face too dark for 8-bit ratios, so front and HDRI decide.

## What each channel does in Iray (variant ÷ base, R G B)

| Variant | forehead | cheek | nose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bce0` Base Color Effect 0 | 1.48 1.75 2.07 | 1.55 1.93 2.61 | 1.51 1.90 2.49 |
| `sssdir0` SSS Direction 0 | 1.00 1.00 1.00 | 1.00 0.98 1.00 | 0.99 1.00 1.00 |
| `w0` Translucency 0 | 2.79 3.30 3.84 | 3.12 3.89 5.29 | 2.88 3.73 4.87 |
| `w09` Translucency 0.9 | 0.36 0.36 0.45 | 0.32 0.20 0.21 | 0.35 0.24 0.27 |

Translucency darkens this skin hard (w 0.5 is about a third of w 0, w 0.9
about a ninth): its volume absorbs (Transmitted 0.98 / 0.38 / 0.38 over
0.3 mm, SSS Amount 0.69 over 0.12 mm). SSS Direction does nothing visible.

- The base skin as it ships (w 0.5, BCE 1) is within 0.83 – 1.18 under the
  front light; under the HDRI blue runs 1.2 – 1.3.
- Without translucency both renderers agree (0.95 – 1.08).
- At w 0.9 Octane is about 2× Iray: the older rule's lift
  1 / (1 − 0.46 · w) grows with w where Iray darkens.
- Base Color Effect 0 (the baked rule) is too red: G and B 0.52 – 0.89.
  Octane brightens the three channels alike (≈ 1.6×), Iray brightens blue
  most (up to 2.6×).
