# 2026-10-29 — OmniHair (#21)

Ratios: Octane / Iray, mean linear luminance (gamma 2.2) over the crown
(x 900–1040, y 300–360 at 1920 × 1080), Octane 1024 samples; hue as g/r and
b/r of the same means, Octane v Iray.

## Where it is used

Five materials in three scenes, all "OmniHair Root-Tip Blend" (DAZ
built-in, `OmniSurface/OmniHairBase.mdl` under DAZ's `shaders/iray`):
Wendy's hair and baby hair (melanin 1, Diffuse Strength 0.8, roughness
0.05), WW2's G9 eyebrows (melanin 0.75 / 1), cell's body hair (melanin
0.55, Highlight 0.3, cutout 0.6 — Enable Opacity off). Before, the export
took them for Iray's dual lobe hair (they have "Hair Root Color") and
wrote albedo 0.046 × white + 0.110 × white: light grey hair.

## The graph

`DzCustomMDLBrick` OmniHairBase fed by the DAZ channels (user parameter
names → MDL arguments):

| DAZ | MDL |
|---|---|
| Melanin (or Melanin Presets 0–6) | melanin_concentration |
| Melanin Redness | melanin_redness |
| Diffuse Strength | base_color_weight |
| Hair Root / Tip Color (blend by root-to-tip) | base_color |
| Highlight Weight | diffuse_reflection_weight |
| Highlight Root / Tip Highlight Color | diffuse_reflection_color |
| Roughness / Roughness Tip | specular_reflection_roughness |
| Azimuthal Roughness (/ Tip), Anisotropic Roughness | azimuthal |
| Specular Reflection Shift | cuticle angle (degrees) |
| IOR | specular_reflection_ior |

OmniHairBase: melanin m → m² · 2.4, split eu = · (1 − r), pheo = · r,
σ = eu · (0.506, 0.841, 1.653) + pheo · (0.343, 0.733, 1.924) — Chiang's
coefficients × 1.21 and × 1.83 (same channel ratios). If the base colour
is not white or its weight not 1, σ = lerp(σ_colour, σ_melanin, √m) with
σ_colour = (ln clamp(c · w · 2.4, 0.001, 1) / f(β))². The roughness goes
through `compute_v` (and `compute_s`) before `df::chiang_hair_bsdf`.

Octane's Hair (147), from a pin probe: `hairMode` (0 Albedo, 1 Melanin +
Pheomelanin), `hairMelanin`, `hairPheomelanin`, `albedo`, `specular`,
`diffuseAmount`, `diffuse`, `ior`, `roughness`, `roughnessV`, `offset`,
randomness pins, `opacity`.

## Scenes

`make-omnihair-scenes.py`: Calib-Hair with the strand material swapped to
OmniHair (URL and channels, strand widths kept): blonde 0.175, brown 0.65,
black 1 (redness 0.5, white), red (0.4, redness 1), dyed (0.3, root/tip
0.8 / 0.3 / 0.2), Wendy's settings, highlight (brown + Highlight 0.5).
Iray: 7 × ~3.5 min local CPU; dumps 10 s each; Octane ~2.5 min each.

## Open

- Octane's pigment model: render pure eumelanin and pure pheomelanin at a
  few concentrations, measure the strand colour, fit Octane's absorption
  per pigment against OmniHair's — then map σ through it. An overnight
  series (≈ 2.5 min per render).
- Highlight (diffuse lobe) 0.73: Octane's diffuse pins are "designed for
  Albedo mode" (manual); unchecked whether melanin mode uses them.
- Specular Reflection Shift → Octane's `offset` (units unknown) left at 0.
