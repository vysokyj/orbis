## Process

- A headless DAZ segfaulted every time a Genesis 8 hair started to follow
  its figure (Kevin, Hudson, Athena); with a window (`HEADLESS=0`) it
  builds. Genesis 9 + Pixie builds headless. The generator sets it.
- DAZ's camera Frame Width (50 mm here) is fitted to the image's height,
  not a 36 mm width: the first framing put the hair at a fifth of the
  frame. The rendered hair takes about 0.6 × the box-height share the
  generator aims for (the box's depth, the head's shape).
- Uber's glossy weight depends on Base Mixing: Glossy Layered Weight in
  the PBR modes, Glossy Weight in Weighted. A -nogloss that set only the
  first left Athena's gloss on (its -nogloss rendered byte-identical).
- The Pixie hair has no glossy layer at all: its gloss is the dual lobe
  specular.
