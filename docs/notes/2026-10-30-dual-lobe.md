## What Iray does (DAZ's MDL)

`DAZStudio.app/Contents/shaders/iray/daz_3d/`:

- `irayubermaterial.mdl`, `add_dual_lobe_specular`: two GGX lobes,
  `weighted_layer(weight: ratio, layer: lobe 1, base: lobe 2)`, over the
  base through a custom curve (normal 1 − r·w, grazing 1 − w).
- `pbr_skin.mdl`: lobe 2's roughness = lobe 1's × Roughness2 (the mult),
  both squared; `weighted_layer(weight: roughness2_weight, layer: lobe 2)` —
  the ratio weighs lobe 2 there.
- Volume (`add_uber_volume`, `pbr_add_volume`): σa = −ln(Transmitted
  Color) / d_t; σs = −ln(SSS Color) / d_s with `logarithmic_scatter_enable`,
  else SSS Amount / d_s. Which of the two DAZ's Uber wrapper picks per SSS
  Mode isn't in the MDL; SSS Reflectance Tint isn't either.

Victoria's (0.98, 0.69, 0.76) scatters little in red (α 0.89 / 0.81 /

direction of every variant, too strongly; (Rd / Rd_base)^0.4 fits them
within 12 % RMS (worst 24 %).
