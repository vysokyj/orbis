# 2026-11-07 — Skin hue after the SSS tint; Faithful skin as Iray's colour (#19)

Picks up #19 after #52 found that DAZ folds the SSS Reflectance Tint into
the diffuse Iray gets (a477397). The question: does `SKIN_HUE` (1 / 0.80 /
0.62, fitted on HDRI and Fetroom scenes before the tint was known) now
count the tint twice? And then, at the user's request: Faithful skin as
close to Iray as it gets, without fitted constants.

Scenes: Calib-Eye, Calib-HDRI (Genesis 8 Female base skin), Calib-Skin-Base
(the same skin on Calib-Skin's body: front lamp, back lamp, HDRI; -w0,
-bce0, -sssdir0) and Calib-Skin's eight figures (front, back, HDRI).
Octane 256 samples, Iray references in the cache (Calib-Skin-Base's in
`iray-full`). Octane / Iray, linear, R / G / B: Calib-Skin-* the median of
the face and body regions (`scripts/dev/skin-compare.py`'s), Calib-Eye /
Calib-HDRI the median over 20 px blocks that `SKIN_HUE` changes by > 8 %.
The figures are store content: numbers only here.

## Which skins `SKIN_HUE` touches

Only the non-baked path: Faithful (Standard Surface, every skin) and
Enhanced's mono SSS with Base Color Effect 1 / 2 — the G8F base skin
(diffuse factor now 1 / 0.85 / 0.63, the tint; w 0.5, effect 1, shown).
Calib-Skin's eight figures in Enhanced take the baked colour and
`SKIN_HUE_OPENPBR`; their faces have effect 0 (Victoria, Monique, …) or a
grey or barely tinted factor (Angel 0.74 grey, Nishi 0.96 / 0.89 / 0.77).

Not a double count: without it the environment-lit skin goes back to the
blue it had before the tint (1.26 – 1.34). But under a lamp it is
right without it. The error depends on the light, not on the material;
one constant can't fit both. `-w0` (no translucency, a plain Universal)
is 0.96 / 1.00 / 0.97 front and 1.05 / 1.05 / 1.05 HDRI: the surface is
right under both, the difference sits in the skin path.

Tried and dropped (Enhanced):
- the G8F skin through the baked path (`skin_albedo`) as is: 1.37 – 1.61
  red, 1.0 – 1.3 G / B;
- the same with Base Color Effect's shares from DAZ's `diffuse_base`
  (diffuse × m / max, translucency 1 − (1 − w)·m): environment 1.05 –
  1.11 / 0.89 – 0.93 / 1.03 – 1.10, but the front lamp 0.90 / 0.62 / 0.54
  (the baked path's fitted hue on top);
- the subsurface radius not square-rooted (G / B 0.39 → 0.16 of red):
  no change (front 0.86 / 0.90 / 0.97, HDRI 1.00 / 1.05 / 1.25).

So Enhanced keeps `SKIN_HUE`: environment light is what most scenes have,
and Enhanced doesn't chase Iray's hue further (Octane over Iray).

## Faithful: where the blue comes from

Faithful (Standard Surface, `skin_tint` × `SKIN_HUE`) was never refitted
on Calib-Skin: front lamp 0.74 – 2.09 in red, Kayo 1.40 / 2.08 / 2.08,
Monique 2.09 / 3.04 / 3.84, Zurie 0.74 / 0.74 / 0.63 (without `SKIN_HUE`
bluer still: Angel 0.88 / 1.15 / 1.42).

The test that turned it: the baked colour (`skin_albedo`, Iray's skin as
the probes measured it) as a **plain diffuse** — OpenPBR, subsurface 0, no
`SKIN_HUE_OPENPBR`, no dark gain. Red came out right on every figure
(0.88 – 1.04) — but green and blue just as blue as before: Victoria 0.99 /
1.27 / 1.60, Monique 1.04 / 1.46 / 1.96, Twosret 0.93 / 1.28 / 1.58. The
blue isn't Octane's random walk; it is in the colour rule: its subsurface
part, `0.55 · Tc² · volume_factor`, is grey on the Genesis 8 volume (the
factor is relative to Victoria's own volume, so 1 / 1 / 1 there).

DAZ's MDL puts that light through the volume (`add_uber_volume`): what it
gives back per channel is the volume's multiple-scattering albedo at its
single-scattering albedo α (σs / (σs + σa), from Transmitted and SSS
Color). On Victoria's volume α = 0.89 / 0.81 / 0.69:

| model | G / R | B / R |
|---|---|---|
| Jensen's dipole Rd(α) | 0.71 | 0.48 |
| √Rd | 0.84 | 0.69 |
| random walk A(α), Chiang et al. 2016 | 0.75 | 0.53 |
| needed (Victoria's plain-diffuse excess) | 0.78 | 0.62 |
| Enhanced's fitted `SKIN_HUE_OPENPBR` | 0.72 | 0.59 |

The fitted hue of the Enhanced bake is this factor — Octane wasn't
bluer, the bake was.

## Process

- A plain diffuse is the cleanest probe of a colour rule: it takes the
  renderer's subsurface out, so what is left is the rule. It found in one
  round what four rounds of figure tuning (#19's history) didn't.
- Calib-HDRI's figure is small in frame: a per-pixel mask from two
  renders' difference picked noise; 20 px blocks worked.
- Renders: 256 samples, ~1 min a scene; nine batches of 5 – 29 scenes.
