# 2026-10-29 — Base Mixing 1 and Morgan Hair (#52)

The blonde in the user's teens scene wears Morgan Hair (PropsChick): cards
on Iray Uber with a cutout map. Iray renders it honey blond with strong
highlights; Octane rendered it near-white and flat.

## The hair

The material, as DAZ evaluates it (a headless script reading every property
of the node's materials):

- Base Mixing 1 (specular / glossiness), Base Color 218 / 207 / 186;
- Glossy Layered Weight 0.98, Glossy Color 240 / 233 / 209, Glossy
  Specular 184 / 179 / 157, Glossy Roughness 0.19 with anisotropy 0.74;
- Top Coat Weight 0.975, Top Coat Color 133 / 126 / 113;
- every Color Effect 2;
- Thin Walled, Refraction Weight 0.106 at IOR 1.79, Translucency 0.059;
- Cutout Opacity map `Morgan Hair TR2.jpg`, `map_gamma` 1 in the scene.

The cutout map's gamma 1 is right: the image carries it, and #49 found that
Iray honours it.

**A small scene for it.** Repeated renders of all of teens froze the Mac
(#50). So Calib-Hair-Teens is a copy of teens in My Library only. Everything
but the blonde is hidden, except her ancestors, and the camera is 90 cm from
her hair. Its first Iray render was empty: with her ancestors hidden (the
dolly, its bone), DAZ hid her too, while the export still wrote her (#53).
Without the building the sun hits her directly; auto-EV set 17.5.

**Iray variants of the hair** (the same EV; × base, linear, on the crown /
side / middle / tips patches):

| variant | ratio to the base |
|---|---|
| Top Coat Weight 0 | 1.13 – 2.09 brighter, and yellower |
| Glossy Layered Weight 0 | highlights gone, side 0.52 – 0.64 |
| all Color Effects 0 | 0.91 – 1.68 |

**Octane against Iray** on those patches: 1.3 – 7.6 ×, bluest on the crown.
On Octane's teens hair, these left it white: Glossy Specular instead of
Glossy Color as `specular`, the IOR from F0, no specular or coating, no
transmission (all within ±5 %).

## Base Mixing 1 in Iray

Calib-SpecMode (generated by `make-calib-scenes.py`, in `calib/scenes`)
has eight Uber spheres in mode 1 at diffuse 0.5, Glossiness 0.7:

| sphere | Iray | Octane before | Octane, F0 as IOR |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 mode 0, refl 0.5 (reference) | glossy | 1.08 | 1.08 |
| S2 Glossy Specular 0 | glossy, dimmer | 1.26 | 1.09 |
| S3 0.2 | glossy | 1.13 | 1.10 |
| S4 0.5 | shiny | 0.74 | 1.08 |
| S5 1 | a mirror | 0.35 | 1.01 |
| S6 0.5 at weight 0.5 | | 0.84 | 1.07 |
| S7 Morgan's gloss | a beige metal | 0.55 | 1.09 |
| S8 S7 + Morgan's top coat | | 0.55 | 1.09 |

So Glossy Specular is the gloss's head-on reflectance F0, with Fresnel to
white at grazing. The export ignored it; it now sets the dielectric IOR from
F0's luminance and tints `specular` with its hue.

- Floored at F0 0.04, S2 stayed at 1.26: black means no head-on
  reflection.
- Reflection as a coating at the F0 IOR instead (specular 0) rendered the
  same, 1.01 – 1.10. The IOR is simpler and leaves the coating to Top Coat.
- DAZ's default Glossy Specular, 0.2314, is F0 0.040: IOR 1.5, as before.
  In teens, 25 of the 223 Uber materials set another value.

289 materials in 41 of the user's scenes are in mode 1, mostly hair and
brows (Makenna, BS French Braid, Gala, BS Short Hair, Morgan).

(Octane / Iray, R / G / B.) The hair has its sheen now, but stays too
bright and too white. What Iray does on top:

- **Top Coat Color through the coat.** Iray darkens and warms what lies
  under the coat: without it the hair is 1.1 – 2.1 × brighter. Octane's
  coating only tints its own reflection (#27).
- **The Color Effects** (all 2) move it by up to 1.7 ×.
- On cards seen edge-on, Fresnel at F0 0.46 leaves little diffuse.
